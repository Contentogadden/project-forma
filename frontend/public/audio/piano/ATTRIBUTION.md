Original project: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

Project Forma uses 17 unmodified MP3 files from C2 to C6. Intermediate pitches
are produced by playback-rate transposition (at most two semitones within this
range). Playback adds gain envelopes and optional low-pass filtering. This is
a compact, single-layer web adaptation, not the full multi-velocity library.
No endorsement by the sample author is implied.
